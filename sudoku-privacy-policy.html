Privacy Policy

App: Sudoku (package com.ldadeveloper.sudoku)
Developer: LDA Developer
Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sudoku app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not require an account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information from the App itself. The App does not include analytics or crash-reporting software operated by us.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves some settings and progress locally on your device so the game works between sessions. This includes your selected language, country, difficulty, stage progress, and remaining lives. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App. Android backup is disabled for the App.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the advertising identifier on your device, along with information such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), app usage, and device details, to show ads and measure ad performance. This information is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites or apps

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your consent choice at any time through the consent options in the App or your device settings. You can also reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads.

4. Rewarded ads

You may choose to watch a rewarded video ad to receive extra lives. Watching is optional. The same AdMob data handling described in section 3 applies to these ads.

5. Permissions

The App uses the following permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check whether a connection is available.
  • Advertising ID and ad services: to show ads through AdMob.

The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or otherwise, contact us so we can address it.

7. Data security

Game data remains on your device. Advertising data is processed by Google according to its security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information the App handles.

8. Third-party services

The only third-party service in the App is Google AdMob (including the Google User Messaging Platform). Their privacy practices are not controlled by us, so please review their policies linked above.

9. Your choices

  • Decline or change personalised ad consent when prompted, or in the App's consent options.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Clear the App's data or uninstall the App to remove all locally stored progress and settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at lda.devops@gmail.com.
