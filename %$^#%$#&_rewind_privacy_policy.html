Privacy Policy for Rewind

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rewind ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the app on Android. Rewind is developed by LDA Developer.

1. Information we collect

Rewind does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.

The app stores the following data only on your device, using Android local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Your unlocked stage progress for each mode (Easy, Medium, Hard)
  • Your best score (high score)
  • Your language and country settings
  • Sound settings
  • Unlimited lives setting

This data stays on your device. We cannot access it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

2. Advertising

Rewind shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and other data to show relevant ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads) or reset your advertising ID there.

3. Permissions

Rewind requests only the permissions needed to show ads and run the game. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

4. Children's privacy

Rewind is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown in the app may be served by AdMob under its child-directed settings.

5. Third-party services

The only third-party service the app uses is Google AdMob. Its handling of data is governed by Google's privacy policy linked above.

6. Data security

Because game data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's security settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes take effect when posted with a new "Last updated" date.
