Who is responsible

Chennemane is published by LDA Developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact lda.devops@gmail.com.

Information stored by the app

Chennemane does not require an account. The app stores your selected age group, language, country, sound preference, and heart balance in the app's local preferences. The age screen asks you to choose an age range; it does not ask for your name, exact date of birth, or contact details. The publisher does not operate a game account or game-data server. Device backup features may copy local app data according to your device and account settings.

Advertising and consent services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to request banner ads and optional rewarded ads, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent messages and provide privacy choices where applicable. These services and their advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, app and ad interactions, ad request information, diagnostics, and consent signals. The information processed and whether it is used for personalized advertising depend on your region, age group, consent choices, device settings, and the providers' services.

Google and participating advertising technology providers may receive information through these SDKs to deliver, measure, secure, and limit advertising, and to manage consent. The app does not directly collect precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising and data disclosures for information about Google's processing, retention, and available controls.

Children and age-appropriate advertising

Chennemane is intended for a general audience that includes children. Before the app requests ads, it asks the user to select one of these age ranges: under 13, 13–17, or 18 or older. The selected range is stored in local app preferences. The app sends the corresponding child, teen, or unspecified age-treatment signal with ad requests; it does not send an exact birth date. Under-13 users receive child-directed ad treatment; users aged 13–17 receive age-restricted ad treatment. Ad requests are restricted to general-audience content. These settings are safeguards and do not replace a parent or guardian's supervision or the publisher's responsibility to follow applicable laws and store policies.

Choices and deletion

You can change the selected age range in Settings. Where UMP privacy options are available, open Settings and choose Privacy options to review or change them. You can clear the app's local data or uninstall the app to remove local preferences. Information processed by Google or other advertising providers is subject to their own retention, access, and deletion practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The publisher will update the date above and publish the current version at the public policy URL used in the app stores and in Chennemane.
